## The Roadmap
 
Here's how I'm thinking about where this goes from here.
 
### Path 1 — Minimal Workshop Paper
 
The easiest version. Submit it as something like:
 
> *Action-conditioned 3D scene-flow prediction for drone navigation using PTv3.*
 
Honestly the current 2-page report is already close to this. Just need cleaner writing, stronger related work, better figures, and ideally swap out the global flow head for a per-point one. No simulation needed — just show the pipeline works end-to-end:

### Path 2 — Strong Workshop Paper

**Probably our best bet!**
 
This is the one I think we should actually want to hit. Same prediction baseline, but add a small experiment showing the model can actually *help choose actions* — not just predict flow into the void.
 
The idea: take a bunch of candidate drone actions, run them through the model to get predicted future point clouds, and pick the one with the least predicted collision risk or most free space. 
Compare against a ground-truth safe/unsafe label from the recorded data.

That's a way stronger story than just reporting EPE.
 
---
 
### Path 3 — Full Paper (Later)
 
If we even want to claim *"our method improves drone navigation performance"* at a top venue, then this need closed-loop experiments — success rate, collision rate, 
path length, proper baselines against FlowNet3D and PointFlowNet. That means Gazebo, AirSim, Isaac Sim, or something similar, which requires more people to work together.
 
Not touching that for now. Way too much scope for where this project is at.
 
---
 
### The Actual Plan
 
For the given time contraint and the upcoming DDL, we're not waiting on simulation. The target is a solid workshop submission:
 
1. **Offline prediction** — EPE/MSE on the real ROS2 drone data

2. **Qualitative viz** — input cloud, ground-truth flow, predicted flow side by side

3. **Ablation** — with vs. without action conditioning

4. **Mini planning demo** — score candidate actions with predicted future clouds

5. **Honest limitations** — no closed-loop yet, future work is MPC-style planning

The framing needs to be *"toward predictive world models for drone navigation"* — not overselling it as solved navigation. That's the honest version and honestly still a meaningful contribution.
